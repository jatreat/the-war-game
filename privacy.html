Privacy Policy

Pine Island War Game — iPhone, iPad, Mac and web. Last updated October 26, 2026.

The short version

The game collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. The app makes no network connections; the whole game is built into it.

What stays on your device

The names you give your generals and your settings (game speed, whether the clock stops while you give orders, the aide-de-camp) are saved on your device only, so they're there the next time you play. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does anything differently.
