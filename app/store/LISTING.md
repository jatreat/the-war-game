## Description

Since 1912, Pine Island Camp has ended every summer with the War Game: two hereditary armies, the Greys and the Blues, attacking and defending the six gates of an invisible town in the Maine woods.

Now you're the general.

ATTACK
Send scouts to count heads at a gate. Post callers to make an unholy racket somewhere else. Then find the weak gate and step in together — "Ready, ready — one, two, STEP!" Outnumber the defenders inside and every eligible man scores.

DEFEND
Forty men, six gates, and it takes eight to plug one. Read the scout reports, shift your squadrons, don't chase noise, and call Lockdown when you're ahead late.

THE REAL RULES
Scoring points, challenge points (triple in a blue flag area), repulses, plugged gates, the whistle and the per-man scoring limits all follow the 2018 Revision of the rules.

EASY TO COMMAND
Tap a gate, choose the job and the number, and the nearest free men go. The clock can stop while you give orders. An aide-de-camp can help run your defense. Classic drag-and-click control is there too, with keyboard shortcuts on the Mac and iPad.

REAL GENERALS
Name your generals yourself, or roll the dice for a real Grey or Blue general from camp history.

• Two days, four quarters: you attack twice and defend twice
• Three AI generals: Camper, Counselor and General
• iPhone, iPad and Mac
• No ads, no accounts, no data collected, plays offline

A tribute to the Pine Island Camp War Game. The War Game belongs to Pine Island Camp and its campers.

## Review notes (paste into App Review Information → Notes)

This app is intended for **unlisted distribution** to the Pine Island Camp community (campers, alumni, staff and families). We will submit an unlisted app request once this version is submitted.

The game is entirely offline and needs no sign-in. To play: pick an army, tap DECLARE WAR, then tap any gate (on the map or the gate buttons along the bottom) to give orders. Each match is four quarters; use the 2× / 4× speed button at the top to move faster.

The general names offered by the dice are real past generals of the camp's War Game, published in the camp's own newsletter, The Pine Needle.

## Unlisted app request

Form: https://developer.apple.com/contact/request/unlisted-app/ (submit after the app has been submitted to App Review).

Suggested answers:
- **Who is the app for?** The community of Pine Island Camp, a boys' summer camp in Belgrade Lakes, Maine, founded in 1902: current campers, alumni, staff and families.
- **Why not public?** It recreates a camp-specific tradition (the camp's annual War Game) and uses the names of real past participants; it's meant to be shared with that community by direct link, not discovered by the general public.
- **How will people get the link?** Through the camp's alumni newsletter, email and social channels.

Apple decides unlisted requests case by case. If it's declined, the app can simply go out as a normal public listing.
